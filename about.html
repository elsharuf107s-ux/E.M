About Me

The Person Behind
the Pixels

Who I am, what I can build, and what I cannot build yet. No percentages, no awards, no client list — I have not earned one.

I'm E.M — a designer and developer who believes in the power of exceptional craft.

I design and build, which for a one-page site is the whole job. There is no handover between a designer and a developer here, because there is nobody to hand it to — the person who draws the page is the person who writes it.

The work is small businesses: trades, food, clinics, shops. What they need is rarely a bigger site — it is a page that says the price, the hours and the phone number without making anyone hunt. My philosophy is not complicated: put the useful thing first, and make it work on a phone in a van.

To be straight with you: this is my first portfolio, and the sites in it are the first I have built. Nobody has paid me for one yet. I would rather you knew that from the page than found it out later — and rather you judged the thirty pages themselves, which you can open right now, than a number I could have typed.

Skills

What I can do, and what I can't yet.

Written plainly, because a percentage bar next to a skill is a number somebody made up. If you need something in the second list, I am not your person yet — and I would rather say so now than three weeks in.

Can do

  • Hand-written HTML and CSS, no framework and no build step
  • Vanilla JavaScript for the things a page actually needs — filters, menus, accordions
  • Layouts that hold from 320px up, checked by measuring rather than guessing
  • WCAG AA colour contrast, real heading order, labelled form fields
  • Designing a page around what a customer came to find
  • Git, and handing over files anyone can pick up

Can't do yet

  • React, Next.js or any framework build
  • Back-end work, databases, or logins
  • E-commerce and payment processing
  • Mobile apps
  • Ongoing hosting and server administration
