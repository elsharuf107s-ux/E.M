About

Money is not the only barrier, but it is the one we can remove.

We fund tuition, but also the things that quietly end degrees — a laptop, a train pass, a bond on a share house, textbooks in first semester. Recipients keep the scholarship for the length of their course as long as they stay enrolled. There is no minimum grade to maintain, because we would rather someone struggled through a hard year than lost their funding in it.

How to apply

Four steps, and we help with all of them.

If any part of this is a barrier, phone us. Helping people through the form is part of the job, not a favour.

  1. Check you are eligible

    First in your family to attend university, enrolled or applying at an accredited institution, and household income under the published threshold. That is the whole test.

  2. Gather three things

    Proof of enrolment or offer, a recent income statement for your household, and one reference from someone who is not a relative. No essay, no portfolio.

  3. Submit the form

    Online or on paper, whichever suits. Paper forms are posted out the same day you ring. Applications are read in the order received.

  4. Interview and outcome

    A twenty-minute conversation, in person or by phone. Outcomes are posted within six weeks, and unsuccessful applicants are told why and invited to reapply.
