Get involved

Three ways to help, all of them needed.

We have no shelter building. Every animal lives in someone's spare room, which is why fostering matters as much as money.

Foster

Take an animal for two weeks or six months. We cover food, vet care and equipment — you cover the couch.

Volunteer

Transport runs, adoption days, laundry, photography. Four hours a month makes a genuine difference.
