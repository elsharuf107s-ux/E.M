Method

Three days, from flour to counter.

The timings are not a flourish. They are why the bread keeps, and why we cannot simply bake more when a queue forms.

Day one

Feed and mix

The starter is refreshed twice. Dough is mixed late afternoon and left to bulk at room temperature until the bench is cold.

Day two

Shape and retard

Shaped into bannetons and put into the cold room for eighteen to twenty-four hours. This is where the flavour actually happens.

Day three

Bake

Oven lit at four, loaded at six-thirty, and run falling through the day — hottest for the first bake, coolest for the last.
