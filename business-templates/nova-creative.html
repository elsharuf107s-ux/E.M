Process

You will always know what week you are in.

  1. Brief

    Ninety minutes, and we write it up. If we disagree with the brief we say so here, not at the presentation.

  2. Routes

    Two directions, properly built. Never three watered-down ones to make the middle look safe.

  3. Build

    The chosen route applied to real artefacts — a pack, a page, a poster — until it holds up.

  4. Handover

    Working files, fonts licensed to you, and a call with whoever inherits it.
