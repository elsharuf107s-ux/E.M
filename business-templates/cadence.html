The platform

Four things, done properly.

Not a suite of forty half-features. The jobs a service business actually loses money on, and nothing else.

Scheduling

Jobs, engineers and vans on one board. Drag to move; the customer gets the new window automatically.

Quoting

Itemised quotes that go out the same day, with a record of when they were opened.

Payment

Card, transfer or a deposit up front. Reconciled against the job, not a separate spreadsheet.

Follow-up

The chase nobody has time for — text, email, review request — queued and sent on its own.
