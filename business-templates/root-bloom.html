How we help

Bring photos. Bring soil. We'll do the rest.

Free, unlimited, and honest — including the part where we tell you your shortlist won't survive that spot.

  1. 1

    Bring photographs and a soil sample

    Aspect, light through the day and drainage decide almost everything. A handful of soil in a bag is enough; we'll test it while you look around.

  2. 2

    We tell you what will live

    Including when your idea won't work here. Far better to hear it now than after you've bought forty plants and dug the bed.

  3. 3

    Choose from what we've grown

    Most stock is raised on site and already acclimatised, rather than arriving soft from a heated warehouse in another climate.

  4. 4

    Plant it — or we will

    Take it home with written care notes, or book the planting service and we'll prepare the beds properly before anything goes in.

  5. 5

    Come back any time

    Advice stays free for as long as you own the garden. If something looks wrong, bring us a leaf.
