Incident response

What happens when something breaks.

A worked example: ransomware detected on an endpoint at 02:14 on a Sunday. This is the actual runbook, not a marketing timeline.

  1. T+00:00

    Detection

    EDR flags anomalous encryption behaviour. The SOC receives the alert automatically — nobody needs to be awake at your end.

  2. T+00:02

    Isolation

    Affected endpoint is network-isolated from the console before an analyst has finished reading the alert. Blast radius stops here.

  3. T+00:09

    Human triage

    On-call analyst confirms scope, checks lateral movement, and pages your named engineer.

  4. T+00:14

    You are called

    We ring you. Not an email into an inbox nobody reads until Monday — a phone call, with a plain summary of what happened and what we've already done.

  5. T+01:30

    Restore

    Clean restore from the most recent verified immutable backup — the one we test every month precisely so this step isn't a gamble.

  6. T+24:00

    Written post-incident report

    Root cause, timeline, what we changed, and what you should change. Delivered whether or not the outcome flattered us.
