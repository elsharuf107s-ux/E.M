Before you read on
Breathe in

Four in · four hold · four out · four hold

Most studios sell intensity. We have never found that it helps people keep coming back.

Serene Spaces holds twelve mats. That number is deliberate — it is roughly the point at which a teacher stops being able to see everyone in the room, and seeing everyone is most of the job.

Classes run to the time on the timetable, but they are not paced against it. If the room needs longer in a posture, the room gets longer. If half of you arrived straight from work and can barely stand up, we will quietly change the plan.

There are no mirrors. There is no music with a beat you are meant to match. Nobody will adjust your body without asking first, every single time.

In case you were wondering

Questions

I have never done yoga. Is that a problem?

No — it is the most common way to arrive. The First Six Weeks course exists precisely so that beginners aren't dropped into a room where everyone else already knows the sequence. You can also come to Morning Practice or Restorative on any day and be entirely fine.

Do I need to be flexible?

Not remotely. Flexibility is an outcome of practising, not a prerequisite for starting, and every posture we teach has an alternative that works from wherever your body currently is.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and yourself. We have mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets to borrow at no charge, and there is no expectation that you own any of it.

Will I be touched or adjusted?

Only if you say yes, and we ask every single time rather than assuming a standing answer. There are consent cards at the door if you would rather signal it silently.

Can I come while pregnant, or with an injury?

Usually yes, and we would like to know beforehand so the teacher can plan alternatives rather than improvise. Email us and we will tell you honestly which classes suit and which don't.

What if I need to leave early?

Then leave early. Sit at the back, take your things with you, and go when you need to. Nobody will comment on it.
