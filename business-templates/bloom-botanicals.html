Our ethos

If we cannot explain why an ingredient is in there, it comes out.

We started because reading a skincare label felt like reading a legal document. Ours fit on the front of the carton. Everything is made in batches of two hundred in Kent, and each jar carries the batch number and the month it was made.

Glass jars come back to us for refilling at a third off. Roughly four in ten do, which is not enough yet, but it is climbing.

“We are a skincare company that would rather sell you fewer, better things less often.”

Ines Bloom, founder
