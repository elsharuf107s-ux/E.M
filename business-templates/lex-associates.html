II

How a matter runs, start to finish.

§ 1

Consultation

Thirty minutes, without charge, in person or by video. You leave knowing whether you have a matter worth pursuing and roughly what it would cost to run.

If the answer is that you don't have a case, we will say so in this meeting rather than three invoices later.
§ 2

Scope and fee

A written engagement letter setting out exactly what is included, who is acting, and what it will cost. Fixed wherever the work can be defined.

Where a fixed fee isn't possible you get an estimate, and we tell you before we approach it.
§ 3

Conduct of the matter

A senior lawyer runs your file. Junior lawyers assist, but they do not lead, and you will always know who holds responsibility for the work.

Your engagement letter names the lawyer acting and gives their direct line.
§ 4

Reporting

Progress updates at agreed intervals, written so that you can act on them without needing a second lawyer to interpret the first one.

You should never have to chase us to find out where your own matter stands.
§ 5

Resolution and closure

Final invoice reconciled against the estimate, an explanation of any difference, and your documents returned in full.

Files are retained for the statutory period; originals are yours on request at any time.

V

Questions we are asked most.

Is the first consultation genuinely free?

Yes — thirty minutes, no obligation and no invoice afterwards. If you don't have a matter worth pursuing we will tell you in that meeting. We would rather lose the file than bill you to reach the same conclusion slowly.

How do your fees work?

Fixed fees wherever the scope can be properly defined, which covers most conveyancing, contract drafting and early-stage family work. Where the work genuinely can't be scoped in advance you receive a written estimate, and we contact you before the work approaches it.

Who will actually handle my matter?

A senior lawyer, named in your engagement letter along with their direct line. Junior lawyers support files but do not run them, and responsibility for the work never quietly moves without you being told.

Will my matter go to court?

Only where that is genuinely the better path. Most matters resolve through negotiation or mediation at a fraction of the cost and time, and we will model both routes for you before you decide.

How quickly can I be seen?

Consultations are usually available within two business days, and same-day where a matter is urgent. Call reception and they will place you with the right lawyer rather than the first one free.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Legal professional privilege applies from your first consultation, including where you decide not to instruct us afterwards. Please don't send confidential detail through the web form — bring it to the meeting instead.
