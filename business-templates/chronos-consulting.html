Services

Four things, done properly.

Every engagement is scoped and priced before it starts, and every one ends with a document your team can act on without us in the room.

  1. Strategy review

    Where the business actually makes money, where it leaks, and which of the three things you are arguing about matters.

    • Margin analysis by line
    • Competitive position
    • Two-year options paper

  2. Operating model

    Structure, decision rights and hand-offs. Usually the answer to "why does everything take so long".

    • Process mapping
    • Role and accountability design
    • Implementation sequence

  3. Commercial due diligence

    For buyers and for boards. Market, customers and the numbers behind the pitch, in three weeks.

    • Customer reference calls
    • Market sizing
    • Red-flag report

  4. Performance improvement

    A costed plan with owners and dates, then we leave. We will come back in six months to check, at no charge.

    • Cost baseline
    • Initiative pipeline
    • Six-month review included
