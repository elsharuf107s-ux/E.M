The price

What is in it, and what is not.

Written out because the industry usually does not. If it is on the left, you have already paid for it.

Included

  • All accommodation, twin or single at the same price
  • Every internal flight, train and transfer on the itinerary
  • A local guide throughout, plus a second on walking days
  • All breakfasts and most dinners, named in the day-by-day
  • Entry to every site listed
  • Tips for guides and drivers

Not included

  • International flights — we will book them for you at cost if you ask
  • Travel insurance, which is a condition of booking
  • Visas, with a written checklist sent on deposit
  • Lunches, so you can eat where you like
  • Drinks other than water with dinner

How we travel

Three rules we have not broken yet.

01

The group stays small

Twelve is the ceiling and eight on some trips. It is the difference between booking a family guesthouse and needing a hotel.

02

The itinerary is the itinerary

Published day by day before you pay, with the driving hours written in. If weather forces a change we tell you why, on the day.

03

The money stays local

Guides, drivers and guesthouses are hired directly in-country. No ground agent takes a cut between you and them.

Enquire

Ask before you book. Everyone does.

A real person answers, usually within a working day, and will happily tell you a trip is wrong for you. Deposits are £300 and refundable for fourteen days.
