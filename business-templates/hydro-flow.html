    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Do you really answer at 3am?

    Yes, and a licensed plumber picks up — not a call centre and not an answering service taking a message for the morning. If we genuinely can't reach you quickly we'll tell you immediately rather than letting you sit and wait.

    What does a call-out cost?

    Nothing. We don't charge to come and assess the problem. You get a written fixed price before any work starts, and if you decide not to proceed there's no charge at all.

    Is there an after-hours surcharge?

    No. A 2am emergency is priced the same as a weekday afternoon. Charging double because someone's pipe burst at an inconvenient hour isn't how we want to run this.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Fully licensed, bonded and insured. Licence numbers appear on every quote and invoice, and we'll email a copy of our insurance certificate on request without any fuss.

    Can you handle an insurance claim?

    Yes. We document the cause with photographs and a written report in the format most insurers ask for, and we can invoice the insurer directly on approved claims.

    What if the problem comes back?

    Workmanship is warranted for twelve months, issued in writing by email. If the same fault returns within that period we come back and fix it at no charge.
