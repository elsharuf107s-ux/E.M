Our story

Rosa came over with a suitcase and a rolling pin.

She opened the first room on Carlton Street in 1998 with nine tables and a menu she never wrote down. Her grandson Matteo runs the kitchen now. The menu is written down these days, but the ragù recipe still is not.

We take bookings for the dining room and the back room seats twenty for private events — birthdays, wakes, engagements. Whatever it is, there will be too much food.

“If the sauce is ready in an hour, you have made something else.”Rosa Bellini, 1932 – 2019
