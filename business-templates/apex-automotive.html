Questions

Asked most often.

Will servicing here void my warranty?

No. You're entitled to have a new car serviced by any qualified workshop provided the correct schedule and specification parts are used. We use OEM or equivalent parts, stamp the book, and keep a digital record you can hand to a dealer.

Do you charge for diagnostics?

Yes, because it's real work — but if you go ahead with the repair we've diagnosed, that diagnostic time comes off the final invoice. You don't pay twice for the same fault.

Can I see the old parts?

Always. We keep everything we've replaced and show it to you at handover. If a part was replaced, you get to see why it needed replacing.

What if you find more than expected?

You get a phone call and a photograph before anything additional is done. We will never widen the job and tell you afterwards — even when it means the car sits on the hoist waiting for you to ring back.

How long is the warranty?

Twenty-four months on our workmanship, plus whatever the manufacturer covers on the parts themselves. It's issued in writing at handover, not mentioned verbally and forgotten.

Do you do performance work on daily drivers?

Yes, and it's most of what we build. We'll be honest about what's worth doing on a car you use every day, including when the answer is that a stage kit will make it worse to live with.
