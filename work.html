Approach

My Process

The same four steps every time. It is short because a one-page site is a small job, and pretending otherwise would only pad the invoice.

01 — Discovery

What do customers actually ring up to ask? That question decides the whole page. I come back with a structure before anything is drawn.

02 — Design

One direction, built properly, rather than three to make the middle look safe. You see it as a real page in a browser, not a picture of one.

03 — Develop

Hand-written HTML and CSS. No framework, no build step, no plugins — so there is nothing to update and nothing to break later.

04 — Launch

Checked on a real phone at 320px up, and checked for contrast and keyboard use. Then the files are yours, to host wherever you like.
